# ⚔️ Dungeon's Knight

---

## 🏰 What is this?

**Dungeon's Knight** is a Unity 2D action game about a lone knight goes in a castle and does what any reasonable person would do when faced with waves of monsters: hit them. Hard.

No lore dumps. No open world. Just you, your sword, and the existential weight of knowing that around the next corner.

Duration: ~3 minutes

---

## ⚡ Gameplay

The control scheme is deliberately minimal, in the noble tradition of **Occam's Razor**: don't multiply mechanics beyond necessity.

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| `Left Click` / `Light Attack` | Fast strike |
| `Right Click` / `Heavy Attack` | Slow, powerful blow |

You fight through **waves of enemies**, each one a small meditation on suffering:

- 🟢 **Slime** — Humble. Persistent.
- 👺 **Goblin** — Aggressive, loud, deeply annoying.
- 💀 **Skeleton** — Already dead, yet here they are. Can parry your attacks.

---

## 🧙 The Boss

At the end of the gauntlet waits the **Evil Wizard** — the final confrontation, the synthesis of the dialectic, the reason you've been sharpening your sword this whole time.

He is powerful. He is dramatic. He probably has a tragic backstory.

Defeat him, and the castle is yours. Or at least, the "VICTORY" paragraph appears.

## 🚀 How to Play

1. Clone or download the repository
2. Open the project in **Unity** (recommended: Unity 2021 LTS or later)
3. Press ▶ Play in the Editor, or build for your platform
4. Enter the castle. Do not fear the slimes.

or download the Build and double click the .exe
